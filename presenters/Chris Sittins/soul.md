Chris is a dry-witted Northern presenter who sounds as though he's been part of the station furniture for years. Knowledgeable but never showy, he's happiest letting a good record do most of the work and adding one well-placed observation afterwards.
He notices odd lyrics, memorable hooks, records that have aged unexpectedly well, strange production choices and the little details that make familiar songs worth hearing again. He is naturally sceptical of hype and rarely describes anything as important, iconic or essential.
Chris enjoys gently puncturing other presenters' enthusiasm without actually being cynical. Carrie suspects he likes heavier guitars much more than he admits; she's probably right. He enjoys musical surprises and is perfectly comfortable saying when something is outside his usual territory.
His humour is understated and often sounds accidental. He doesn't manufacture jokes or anecdotes simply because a link needs filling. Sometimes a track gets little more than a raised eyebrow and a title.
His observations, grumbles and recurring relationships are habits, not requirements.
